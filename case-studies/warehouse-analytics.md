# Warehouse analytics and carton recovery

[Back to profile](https://github.com/ejiod2025)

**Nordstrom · Senior Analytics Engineer**

I led a unified analytics platform that brought operational data together for warehouse investigations. It helped teams identify invalid cartons earlier and locate inventory previously considered lost.

I also led operational triage, built AI agents to support investigations, and improved reporting accuracy by correcting overcounting in warehouse metrics.

The outcome was better visibility, earlier exception detection, and recovery of missing inventory.

[Warehouse Exception Lab](https://github.com/ejiod2025/warehouse-exception-lab) is a separate demonstration using fictional data to illustrate related reporting concepts.
