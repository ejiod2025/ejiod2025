## The work

My SiriusMed work connected applied AI with healthcare workflows. It included a voice-enabled electronic medical record, a WhatsApp medication-adherence prototype, and Yoruba speech-recognition development.

For speech recognition, I worked with Python, PyTorch, and Whisper, including audio preprocessing, experiment tracking, and evaluation using word error rate (WER) and character error rate (CER).

## Engineering considerations

**Recognition quality needs measurement.** WER and CER make transcription errors visible and provide a basis for comparing experiments.

**The workflow matters alongside the model.** Voice input, transcription, and the action a user takes afterward need to fit together. The medication-adherence prototype explored how this communication workflow could support users.

**Failure cases are useful development inputs.** Speech recognition can fail on pronunciation, background noise, or domain-specific terms. Reviewing these errors helps connect model evaluation to the experience of the person using the system.
