# Portfolio Reflection

I organized my GitHub portfolio around the work I want employers to understand first: building reliable data platforms and using them to solve operational problems. The introduction connects my current role at Top Supply Chain Industry with platform modernization, data integration, and warehouse analytics. I placed the resume and LinkedIn links near the top so visitors can find my background and contact me quickly.

I shortened the case studies to make my contribution and its purpose clear. The platform overview describes moving legacy infrastructure to cloud native platforms. The warehouse overview connects engineering to earlier carton issue detection, inventory recovery, and accurate reporting. The AI overview adds my experience with voice workflows and speech evaluation. Using an industry label keeps the focus on my work while keeping my current employer unnamed. I also distinguish completed work from prototypes and architecture evaluations.

The Warehouse Exception Lab gives visitors something they can run. Its fictional data, examples, and tests demonstrate how I approach warehouse reporting and data quality without sharing employer systems or records.

My next step is to extend this demo with evaluations for AI-assisted investigations: checking whether an assistant cites the right events, recognizes incomplete evidence, and requests human review when needed. This would connect my data-engineering experience with the AI skills I am developing through graduate study and give visitors a concrete way to assess the results.
